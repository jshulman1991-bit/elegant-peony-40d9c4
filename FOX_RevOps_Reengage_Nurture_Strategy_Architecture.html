FOX RevOps Reengage & Nurture ArchitectureThe guardrails
05  /  Trust & guardrails

Why re-engagement never embarrasses us.

Talking to someone who already knows FOX is high-risk: one wrong fact and the relationship is gone. Two mechanisms make that safe — an accuracy loop on every message, and hard gates that decide who never gets contacted.

Mechanism 1

The accuracy loop

Non-optional for any email that references prior history.

1
Draft on the record, not from thin air
Grounded in the contact’s real activity & deal history — never freeform.
2
Run the AI accuracy check
The draft is tested against what we actually know.
3
Feed the result back
“Ensure the email is as accurate as possible” — corrections applied.
4
Confirmation pass
A dedicated accuracy review before anything is staged.
5
Manual send in Outreach
Lands as a manual step — the rep reads it and sends.

“One hallucinated fact in a re-engagement torpedoes trust. This loop is non-optional.”

FOX RevOps operating doctrine
Mechanism 2

Stop-and-ask gates

Checked before enrollment and on every touch. If a gate trips, the prospect is held — not sent to.

Unsubscribed or engine-excluded
unsubscribed_globally or prospect_engine_excluded → never enroll
Now a current member
Route to the member side — out of prospecting entirely
Contacted 3× this quarter
reengagement_attempts_q ≥ 3 → defer to next quarter (the cooldown)
8× this year
reengagement_attempts_y ≥ 8 → surface for rep review
Invalid or bouncing email
Never enroll — routed to the data-fix queue
Missing country, or dropped < 30 days
Surfaced for a human decision, not auto-enrolled
Live health monitors
After a sequence activates, the engine watches the numbers and pulls back on its own: bounce > 5% → pause, reply < 2% → revise the copy, opt-out > 2% → stop. The system protects the sender reputation so your name stays clean.

06  /  The cast

Software does the heavy lifting; people make the calls.

A conductor dispatches the work to four specialist agents. They find, clean, score and draft — then hand off to Jake to activate and to you to approve and send. No agent ever sends on its own.

The Conductor
fox-revops-orchestrator
Reads the request, classifies the work, and dispatches it to the right specialist. The single front door to the whole system.
The Router
inbound-processor
Takes a new lead from message → enrichment → CRM → Outreach, assigns the owner and stages the first reply against the time-to-first-touch SLA.
The Housekeeper
hubspot-hygiene
Keeps the CRM honest — flags deals stalling at 30 / 60 / 90 days and splits a book into custom-email, sequence-only and hold for re-engagement.
The Writer
sequence-writer
Designs the sequences and writes the copy through the accuracy loop — then stages it for Jake to activate. Never auto-activates, never freeforms.
The human handoff
Agents find, clean, score & draft → Jake activates the sequence → Mel / Dave / Miguel approve & send every email → Prospect replies → rep takes the call

FOX RevOps Reengage & Nurture ArchitectureThe craft
08  /  The craft

How every email is written.

Two things earn the right to talk to someone who already knows FOX: it has to be accurate, and it has to sound like you. Both are enforced before anything reaches your queue.

The rules of the house

Enforced on every draft

One clear ask per email — a single, low-friction next step
Grounded in the real record — never an invented fact
A short, specific subject line
Signed “Best Regards, Mel Simms”
No “just checking in” or “circling back”
No “pitch,” “sell,” “unlock value,” “synergy”
No exclamation points, no emojis
Anatomy of a first touch  Illustrative

From:  Mel Simms, FOX
Subject:  What’s changed at FOX since 2024

When your family office stepped away from FOX in 2024, the peer network looked different than it does now.

Opens on the shared history, not “just checking in”

Two things are new that line up with what you were weighing then: a direct-investing peer group and the operating-family cohort.

One specific value angle, grounded in the record

Best Regards,

Your sign-off, applied automatically
It always sounds like the right person
Each rep has their own signature and rules, applied automatically: Dave signs “Best, Dave Blide”; Miguel writes in his voice and never includes a scheduling link. You never inherit someone else’s style — and you always have the final edit.
